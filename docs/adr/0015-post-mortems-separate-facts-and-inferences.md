# Post-mortems separate facts and inferences

SENTINEL post-mortems distinguish direct facts from inferred explanations and proposed action items. Timelines, detected impact, executed remediations, approvals, and cited evidence must come directly from investigation state, while root cause and contributing factors may include evidence-backed inferences with confidence levels.

**Considered Options**

- Generate a polished freeform post-mortem from the final diagnosis
- Restrict post-mortems to raw timelines and evidence only
- Separate facts, inferences, and proposed action items in the post-mortem

**Consequences**

Post-mortems become more auditable and less likely to invent certainty under narrative pressure. The documentation workflow must preserve which statements are factual, which are inferred, and which follow-up actions still need human acceptance.
